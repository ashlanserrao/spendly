Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Service, why we collect it, and the choices you have over it.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service:

  • Account details — the name, email address, and password you provide when you register. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter yourself.
  • Basic technical data — information your browser sends automatically, such as IP address and browser type, used to keep you signed in and to diagnose errors.

We do not connect to your bank, and we never ask for card numbers, account numbers, or other payment credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Display your expenses back to you and generate category and monthly summaries.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Diagnose faults, prevent abuse, and improve how the Service works.
  • Send essential account messages, such as password resets.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or use your expense records to target advertising at you.

3. Data Storage

Your records are stored in our application database and retained for as long as your account is active. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect them against loss and unauthorised access.

You can edit or delete individual expenses at any time from within your account. Deleting your account removes your personal data from our active systems; residual copies may persist briefly in routine backups before being overwritten.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we encourage you to keep independent copies of any records that matter to you.

4. Third Party Services

We keep external dependencies to a minimum. Where we do rely on third parties — for example hosting providers, or web font delivery — those providers may process limited technical data such as your IP address in order to serve the site.

Any such provider is used only to operate Spendly and is not permitted to use your information for its own purposes. We do not run third party advertising or analytics trackers on your expense data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or you would like to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, get in touch at privacy@spendly.example.

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date shown at the top of this page. Related terms are set out in our Terms and Conditions.
